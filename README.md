# Quantitative Research Portfolio

**MSc Social Research Methods and Statistics · University of Manchester**

## Portfolio statement

This portfolio brings together applied statistical research from my MSc, highlighting [longitudinal data analysis](projects/elsa-loneliness/README.md), [logistic regression](projects/ess9-statistical-modelling/README.md#part-1--household-income-and-crime-victimisation-in-great-britain), [multilevel modelling](projects/ess9-statistical-modelling/README.md#part-2--household-income-and-internet-use-across-countries), [interpretation of model results](projects/ess9-statistical-modelling/MODEL_TO_INTERPRETATION.md), and [critical review of modelling decisions](docs/elsa-audit.md). These skills support evidence-led research and data analysis roles.

## Data & transparency

The portfolio distinguishes **submitted coursework results** from **later independent re-analysis**. Some data are access-controlled and are not published here. The ELSA results were independently re-checked during portfolio preparation, while the prepared R script remains unverified in an R environment; the ESS9 results have not yet been independently reproduced. I do not claim that the reported associations establish causal effects.
